INTERPRETATION / VERIFICATION / LIMITS

3. What the experiment isolates

Trailing-average and forecast order-up-to rules set target quantities and scale them to budget. Point optimization allocates the same budget using one forecast path and a cost objective. Scenario optimization uses that same objective with forecast-plus-residual paths. All policies share stock, lead time, budget and evaluation sales within each episode.

Point optimization costs $678.05 less than trailing-average orders in aggregate, but wins only 8/17 windows. It beats scenario optimization in 14/17 windows. This supports investigating the current scenario construction; it does not prove uncertainty-aware optimization is generally inferior.

4. Prediction and uncertainty

Gradient boosting has daily MAE 1.645 versus 1.712 for the trailing mean, approximately 3.95% lower. Twelve earlier residual blocks support eight sampled paths. The daily 10–90% scenario envelope contains 52.42% of evaluation observations; it is not a calibrated 80% prediction interval.

5. What was verified

Independent daily replay of saved orders matched all 68 policy-window costs, with maximum absolute difference below $0.000001. Nonnegative integer orders, budget feasibility, unmet units and ending stock were checked. This is output-accounting verification, not independent retraining from official raw files.

6. Boundaries of the conclusion

This is an exploratory retrospective comparison, not a fresh holdout. It includes previously inspected periods; shared histories and seasons make observations dependent. Each episode resets initial stock. Only 3/42 series overlap V1, so differences between the two studies cannot be attributed to a method revision.

Observed sales may understate demand. Purchase cost, holding cost, shortage penalties and initial stock are assumed. Ending stock receives no salvage credit. No statistical significance, deployment readiness or causal business benefit is claimed.

Stopping decision: close this bounded experiment, preserve negative findings and avoid further model search merely to obtain a favorable headline. Any operational adoption requires verified inputs and prospective evaluation.
